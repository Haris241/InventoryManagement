What Is a Fiscal Year?

A fiscal year is the twelve-month window your business reports on — it doesn't have to match the calendar year, and in Harvora ERP, it's set once at registration and reused every year after.

info Why Fiscal Years Exist

Every accounting report — Trial Balance, Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet — has to cover a defined period, not "all time." A fiscal year is that period: exactly twelve months, after which your books close, profit or loss is calculated, and a fresh period begins.

Not every business runs January to December. A retailer might close its year around its slow season; a farm might align with harvest; some countries set a standard fiscal year by law that's different from the calendar year. Because of this, Harvora lets each business choose its own starting month instead of forcing January.

calendar_month How Your Fiscal Year Is Set Up

You choose your starting month once, during registration. Every fiscal year you create afterward — this one and every future one — follows that same starting month automatically.

You choose at registration: Starting month — e.g. July
Harvora sets: Fiscal Year 2026 = 1 Jul 2026 → 30 Jun 2027
Next year, automatically: Fiscal Year 2027 = 1 Jul 2027 → 30 Jun 2028
  • Start and end dates are calculated for you — you never enter them manually.
  • When creating a new fiscal year, you only provide a Name and optional Remarks.
  • Once created, a fiscal year's details can't be edited — only closed, switched to, or deleted.

layers More Than One Active Year

You're not limited to a single open year. Harvora allows more than one fiscal year to stay Active at the same time — useful when you're closing out one year's books while already posting transactions in the next.

sync_alt Switching Your Logged-In Year

The fiscal year you're logged into controls two things: which year new vouchers post to, and which year's data your dashboard shows. This is separate from the calendar date — your logged-in year is whatever you last switched to here, regardless of today's actual date.

Today's date: 2026
Want to review or post into 2022? Switch your logged-in year to 2022 from this list.
Dashboard and new vouchers now reflect 2022 until you switch back.

rule Rules to Remember

  • A fiscal year can be deleted only if it has zero transactions posted against it.
  • Once created, a fiscal year's name and remarks can't be edited.
  • Posting into a Closed year sets its status to Needs Reclosure until you close it again.
  • Always confirm which year you're logged into before posting — it determines where your entry lands.
