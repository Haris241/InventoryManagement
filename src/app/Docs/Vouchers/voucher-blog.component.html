Voucher Manager Guide

A voucher is how every transaction gets recorded in Harvora ERP — it's the source document behind every entry in your Chart of Accounts. Some vouchers you post yourself; others are created automatically when a purchase or sale happens.

balance The One Rule Every Voucher Follows

Harvora ERP supports multi-currency transactions, so two lines on the same voucher can be entered in different currencies with different exchange rates — their transaction-currency Debit and Credit amounts won't always match line to line. What must always match, on every voucher without exception, is the total Base Debit and total Base Credit — both converted into your organisation's base currency. If those two totals aren't equal, the voucher can't be posted.

edit_note Journal Voucher User-Posted

The most flexible voucher type. Use it for adjustments, accruals, corrections, and any transaction that doesn't fit a cash, bank, or contra entry. A Journal Voucher can have any number of lines, each debiting or crediting a different account, in any currency.

checkAdd as many lines as the transaction needs.
checkEach line can debit or credit any account and any currency.
checkBase Debit total must equal Base Credit total before posting.

flag Opening Voucher User-Posted

Used once, when you're setting up your accounts — to bring in balances carried over from your previous system or bookkeeping method. It behaves like a Journal Voucher: multiple lines, free-form debits and credits, balanced in base currency.

checkSame multi-line, multi-currency structure as a Journal Voucher.
checkTypically used during setup or migration, not day-to-day operations.
checkStill must balance in base currency like every other voucher.

payments Cash Payment Voucher User-Posted

Records cash leaving the business — paying an expense, a supplier, or anything else out of hand cash. The first line is always the cash account, and Harvora automatically credits it since a payment reduces your cash balance. Every other line is a debit — the accounts the money went toward.

CreditLine 1 — Cash account (fixed, reduces on payment)
DebitAll other lines — expense or other accounts

savings Cash Receipt Voucher User-Posted

Records cash coming into the business. The first line is the cash account and is automatically debited, since a receipt increases your cash balance. Every other line is a credit — where the money came from.

DebitLine 1 — Cash account (fixed, increases on receipt)
CreditAll other lines — income or other source accounts

account_balance Bank Payment Voucher User-Posted

The bank equivalent of a Cash Payment Voucher — for money leaving through a bank account instead of cash in hand. The first line is the bank account and is automatically credited, since a payment reduces the bank balance. Every other line is a debit.

CreditLine 1 — Bank account (fixed, reduces on payment)
DebitAll other lines — expense or other accounts

account_balance_wallet Bank Receipt Voucher User-Posted

For money received into a bank account. The first line is the bank account and is automatically debited, since a receipt increases the bank balance. Every other line is a credit.

DebitLine 1 — Bank account (fixed, increases on receipt)
CreditAll other lines — income or other source accounts

swap_horiz Contra Voucher User-Posted

For moving money between your own cash and bank accounts — depositing cash into the bank, or withdrawing cash from it. Because it only ever moves money within the same business, a Contra Voucher is always exactly two lines, and the account dropdown only offers Cash and Bank accounts.

checkExactly two lines — no more, no fewer.
checkAccount choices are restricted to Cash and Bank accounts only.
checkOne line debits, the other credits — money simply moves between the two.

shopping_cart Purchase Voucher Auto-Posted

Created automatically whenever you record a purchase — you won't find a "new voucher" option for this type in Voucher Manager. It exists here so you can review, search, and trace purchase transactions alongside every other voucher in your books.

sell Sales Voucher Auto-Posted

Created automatically whenever a sale is recorded, the same way a Purchase Voucher is generated from a purchase. It appears in Voucher Manager for review and reporting, not for manual entry.
