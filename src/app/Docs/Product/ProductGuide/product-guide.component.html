What Is a Product in an Inventory System?

A product is the master record for anything you buy, stock, or sell — it defines what the item is, how it's priced, and how it's tracked in stock. Everything else in Harvora ERP that touches inventory, from a GRN to a sales invoice, ultimately points back to a product record.

lightbulb Why Getting Product Setup Right Matters

A messy product catalogue — duplicate SKUs, missing costs, inconsistent variants — quietly breaks everything downstream: stock counts drift, margins get miscalculated, and reports stop being trustworthy. Setting products up correctly once, with clean variants and accurate costs, is what makes every later report and transaction reliable.

badge Product Basics

  • Product Name and Product Number are both required and must be unique across your catalogue.
  • UOM, Product Type, and Product Category are required for every product.
  • Brand is optional, but if provided it must be a valid, existing brand.
  • Every product needs at least one variant — there's no such thing as a product with zero variants.

style Variants

A variant is a specific, sellable version of a product — if "Cotton T-Shirt" is the product, "Cotton T-Shirt, Red, Medium" is a variant. Simple products with no real variations just get a single variant. Each variant is tracked, priced, and stocked independently.

  • Every variant needs a unique SKU — unique both within the product and across your entire catalogue.
  • Cost Price and Selling Price are required for every variant and must be greater than zero.
  • Two variants can't share the exact same combination of attribute values — you can't create "Red + Medium" twice on the same product.
  • Attribute values must actually belong to the attribute you're assigning them under.

stacks Opening Stock

While creating a product, you can optionally set an opening quantity for each variant in one or more warehouses — useful when you're bringing existing inventory into Harvora for the first time.

  • Opening stock is entered per variant, split across one or more warehouses if needed.
  • Each entry needs a Warehouse, a Quantity greater than zero, and a Unit Cost greater than zero.
  • You can't enter the same warehouse and location twice for the same variant.

Opening stock is a one-time entry made only when the product is created. Once saved, it can't be edited or removed from the product screen — any later stock correction should go through a stock adjustment, not by changing the product record.

edit Editing a Product

Product details stay editable, but existing variants are locked once created — this keeps your stock and transaction history consistent with what was actually bought and sold.

  • Name and Number remain unique when updated, the same as on creation.
  • Existing variants — SKU, prices, and their opening stock — can't be modified after creation.
  • New variants can be added to an existing product at any time.

verified_user Created as One Unit

The product, its variants, and any opening stock are all saved together as a single operation. If any part fails — an invalid warehouse, a stock error — nothing is saved partially. You'll get a clear error and can fix it and try again, without ending up with a half-created product.
